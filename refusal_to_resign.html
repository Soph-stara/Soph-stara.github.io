"""

Resignation and the Refusal to Resign is a kinetic textile installation that stages the tension between entropy and resistance. Using robotic motors to repeatedly stretch and release fabric, the work makes tangible the oscillation between resignation (release) and the refusal to resign (stretch).

""" def concept(self):

The Second Law of Thermodynamics tells us that entropy (disorder) will always increase in an isolated system. Yet hope persists. This contradiction does not have to be resolved, but is a state to inhabit. Drawing on Schrödinger's concept of negentropy, the installation enacts resistance through continuous energy input: linear actuator motors function as agents of order, temporarily defying material collapse.

This act of defiance, of remaining within contradiction rather than resolving it, is what animates the piece.

def installation(self):

The textile structure is mounted diagonally on the wall at approximately two meters, tilting downward. Viewers stand directly beneath the suspended installation, placing themselves within its field of movement rather than observing from a distance.

yield "Linear actuator motors provide sustained mechanical force" yield "The fabric gradually shows signs of strain and fatigue" yield "The system remains intentionally unstable and imperfect" yield "Audible and tactile: friction, mechanical whirring, tightening" yield "Spatial arrangement reinforces precarity and endurance"
def experience(self):

The angled suspension generates bodily awareness of looming pressure, as if the system could collapse at any moment. Through this tension between mechanical persistence and material vulnerability, the viewer experiences cognitive dissonance: the knowledge that decay is inevitable, and yet the system continues.
